How the groups were formed

  1. Ballots by method and turnout for each precinct in the four elections on the current map.
  2. Each measure compared within its own election, so presidential-year turnout doesn't drive the groups.
  3. Each precinct's profile averaged across the four elections.
  4. Precincts grouped with k-means; the number of groups chosen with the elbow, silhouette and gap tests.
  5. Age added afterward to describe the groups.

Choosing the number of groups

Spread within groups falls steeply until five groups, then levels off.

Fit and robustness by number of groups

Higher is better except within-group spread. Agreement runs from 0 (chance) to 1 (identical groups).

Definitions and assumptions
Turnout
Ballots ÷ registered voters. Nov 2026 registration from the Nov 3 turnout export.
ED / AV / Early Voting
Election Day, Absentee and Early Voting ballots as shares of all ballots.
Consistency
How often each election, on its own, places the precinct in its group: Stable = all 4 elections, Mostly stable = 3, Shifts = 2 or fewer.
2028 registration
Nov 2026 registration grown at each precinct's 2024–2026 rate.
2028 base
Nov 2026 turnout and ballot mix, shifted by the group's 2024-vs-2026 change.
Lower turnout
Base scaled to the lowest presidential turnout since 2004.
Early Voting growth
Five more points of ballots cast through Early Voting. A what-if.
Not included
Individual voter records, outreach history, and precinct data before the 2024 redraw.
